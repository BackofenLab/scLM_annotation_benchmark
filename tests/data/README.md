# Toy dataset

`toy_blood.h5ad` holds 40 cells of the Human Immune Health Atlas (Gong et al. 2025), 10 of each of
four cell types: naive CD4-positive T cells, classical monocytes, CD56-dim natural killer cells and
naive B cells. The cells come from the 1,000-cell test set of the benchmark, which was drawn from
CELLxGENE Census release 2025-11-08 (dataset `e522d2cd-7927-4e59-a4ed-064009569279`). The file
holds the raw counts of the genes detected in at least one of the cells, the cell type labels
(Cell Ontology terms) and the batch, donor, assay and tissue of each cell.

The data are licensed under CC BY 4.0 by the authors of the atlas.

Gong Q, Sharma M, Glass MC, et al. Multi-omic profiling reveals age-related immune dynamics in
healthy adults. Nature. 2025. doi:10.1038/s41586-025-09686-5
